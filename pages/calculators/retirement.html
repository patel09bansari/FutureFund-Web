Planning Your Retirement

The Silent Killer: Inflation

If your current expenses are ₹50,000/month, in 30 years at 6% inflation, you will need ₹2.87 Lakhs/month just to maintain the exact same lifestyle.

  • This is why leaving retirement money in a Savings Account (3% return) guarantees you will run out of money.
  • Your investments MUST beat inflation.
The 25x Rule (4% Rule)

How do we calculate the "Corpus Needed"? We use the 25x Rule (or 4% Safe Withdrawal Rate).

  • Find your annual expenses at the time of retirement.
  • Multiply that by 25.
  • If you withdraw 4% of this corpus every year, historical data suggests it will last you at least 30 years in retirement.
