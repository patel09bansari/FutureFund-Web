Goal-Based Investing

Why Set Specific Goals?

Investing aimlessly often leads to panic selling during market dips. By associating money with a specific goal, you add a psychological safety net.

  • Clarity: You know exactly how much to save each month.
  • Asset Allocation: A 2-year goal requires safe Debt instruments (e.g. FDs, Liquid Funds). A 10-year goal requires volatile Equity (e.g. Index Funds) to beat inflation.
The Inflation Trap

A ₹10 Lakh wedding today will cost ₹17 Lakhs in 10 years (assuming 6% inflation).

  • Always adjust your target amount for inflation before calculating your monthly required savings.
  • If you save in a bank account giving 3% while inflation is 6%, your purchasing power is actually decreasing every year.
The Mathematics Behind It

To find the Required Monthly Savings (PMT), we reverse the Future Value of Annuity formula, while accounting for the growth of any Current Savings (PV).

PMT = [FV - PV(1 + r)^n] × [r / ((1 + r)^n - 1)]

FV = Future Target Amount
PV = Present Value (Current Savings)
r = Monthly Rate of Return (Annual / 12 / 100)
n = Total Months (Years × 12)
