The Magic of Compounding

Why it's called "Magic"

Compound interest is interest calculated on the initial principal AND the accumulated interest from previous periods.

  • Exponential Growth: Your money grows on a curve, not a straight line. The longer you wait, the steeper the curve.
  • Time > Rate: Giving your money 10 extra years to grow is often more powerful than finding a fund with 2% higher returns.
Compounding Against You

Just as compounding builds wealth in investing, it destroys wealth in debt (like Credit Cards).

  • Credit Card Debt: Often compounds monthly at 36-40% annually. This is why minimum payments keep you trapped forever.
  • Inflation: Inflation is reverse-compounding. At 6% inflation, the purchasing power of your money halves every 12 years.
The Mathematics Behind It

The classic compound interest formula:

A = P (1 + r/n)^(nt)

A = Final Amount (Total Maturity)
P = Principal (Initial investment)
r = Annual interest rate (decimal)
n = Number of times interest is compounded per year
t = Time (Years)
