Understanding SIPs

When to use an SIP?

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) is ideal for salaried individuals to build long-term wealth without needing to time the market.

  • Rupee Cost Averaging: You buy more units when markets are low and fewer when markets are high.
  • Discipline: Automating a fixed amount every month prevents emotional investing.
  • Compounding: The earlier you start, the harder your money works for you.
Common Mistakes
  • Stopping SIPs during market crashes: This is when you acquire units the cheapest! Stopping defeats the entire purpose of an SIP.
  • Unrealistic return expectations: While Indian equities have historically given 12-14%, expecting 20%+ consistently is dangerous.
  • Not stepping up: As your salary grows, your SIP amount should grow (Step-up SIP).
The Mathematics Behind It

An SIP is essentially a Future Value of an Annuity calculation. The formula used by this calculator is:

FV = P × [ ((1 + r)^n - 1) / r ] × (1 + r)

FV = Future Value (Total Value)
P = Monthly SIP Amount
r = Monthly rate of return (Annual Rate / 12 / 100)
n = Total number of months (Years × 12)
